GUIDED ARROW v1.3.0
- Rebuilt Guided Arrow Mastery as a centre-outward specialization tree.
- Raised maximum mastery rank to 99 with one mastery point per rank.
- Converted all 19 masteries to multi-level skills, generally with 10 or 20 levels.
- Added current-level, next-level, prerequisite-level and maximum-level information to every node.
- Added a Bannerlord-scaled XP curve, unique-victim rewards and a 32 XP cap per shot.
- Added a Mastery XP Multiplier setting from 0.25 to 3.00.
- Migrated old binary unlocks to level 1 of their corresponding masteries.
- Removed progression patches from the normal Guided Arrow MCM getters, fixing the crash when that page was opened with progression enabled.
- Applies mastery limits only during Guided Arrow mission callbacks and restores the original MCM values immediately afterwards.
- Fixed Guided Release level 1 to enforce a real four-second guidance cap.
- Added per-level scaling for guidance, turn control, time dilation, autoguidance, splitting, formations and penetration.
- Preserved all tested v1.2.2 character-screen, penetration-stability and additive native/TOR-volley fixes.
